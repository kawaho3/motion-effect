MOTION EFFECT - IDENTITY LOCK V6 - MAX AND MIA
===============================================
Problem
-------
The user's two images demonstrate an unacceptable change in facial identity
between the idle/silent and speaking states. In the earlier v5 code, mouth
repainting was applied over a 97x65 face patch, and listening characters
were given a different gaze based on whether they were speaking. At close
range, the face could appear to change when dialogue starts or ends.

Fix
---
Both characters use their EXACT same transparent original standing PNG
for every viseme. All mouth art is composited through one small, stable,
feathered mask centered at the original mouth. Pixels outside a bounded
mouth box are byte-for-byte unchanged between idle and speaking, including
hair, cheeks, eyebrows, eyes, nose, ears, shoulders, skin and shadow.
The alpha/silhouette is restored from the source and never changed.

v6 retains the existing v5 speech visemes: rest, M/B/P closed, soft,
E, A, O/U and F/V. The Kokoro ONNX speech engine, voice names, timings,
2-minute soundtrack, scene transitions and 12 locations remain intact.

Identity differs from expression. Eyes may still blink on a short,
independent schedule. A blink is NOT triggered by a dialogue turn. Voice-
triggered automatic gaze shifts are removed so the character's eyes do not
snap into a different drawing when they begin talking. Both characters
use exactly the same identity-lock policy.

Implementation
--------------
episodes/vegetable_shop/identity_lock.py - local mouth-only compositor
episodes/vegetable_shop/mouth_rig.py - existing v5 art-generation engine
episodes/vegetable_shop/journey.py - calls locked_mouth_variant rather
                                    than painting large face regions
episodes/vegetable_shop/identity_report.py - pixel-invariance checks + atlas
tests/test_identity_lock_v6.py - reproducible two-character tests

WAV, phoneme timeline and subtitles inside episodes/vegetable_shop/output.
See docs/SOUND_GUIDE_KOKORO.txt; no paid TTS service is necessary.

Quality gates
-------------
1. Same original source image dimensions and alpha for all seven poses.
2. Exactly ZERO changed pixels outside mouth rectangle for both characters.
3. The eye and cheek pixels are identical between idle and speech poses.
4. Returning to idle restores the original face byte-for-byte.
5. A distinct speech mouth appears on both characters.
6. No forced eye or head swap at a speech start.
7. Keep the visual comparison a human review step. Passing pixel tests
   cannot certify that a painted mouth looks as natural as the reference.

Important limitation
--------------------
This fixes inconsistency of the *identity* between idle and speaking, NOT
all realism issues. The current hand-painted procedural mouth shapes are
still not a professional facial rig. The next quality step is to replace
v5 procedural visemes with small, artist-aligned mouth patches generated
against these same original Max and Mia faces, never a separately generated
portrait. Preserve this mask and the invariance tests in that upgrade.
