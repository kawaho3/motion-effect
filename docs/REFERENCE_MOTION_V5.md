# Reference motion match — Vegetable Adventure v5

**User-supplied reference**: `Morning_English_A_Rose_in_the_Garden (1).mp4`, 20.000 s, 1920×1080, 24 fps. Video was inspected directly. It is **not included** in this public repository; the reference's actual character artwork/audio is not redistributed or inserted into the vegetable animation.

## Frame-by-frame observations

From the adult speaker during approx. 4.25–7.0 seconds:

- **4.250 s:** large, dark open oral cavity, lips stretched relatively wide
- **4.375–4.625 s:** strong toothy/closed-smile alternation, while the face stays otherwise stable
- **4.750 s:** mouth nearly closed
- **5.000–5.250 s:** open oval, with dark cavity and changing teeth/upper edge
- **5.625–5.875 s:** closes and relaxes toward smiling line
- **6.125–6.500 s:** teeth visible with flatter, wider lip pose
- **6.625–7.000 s:** returns to large dark open mouth
- A brief blink is visible around 5.1–5.4 s. The eye remains mostly still between blinks.

These observations establish an apparent **limited sprite/keyframe mouth set**, updated on the cadence of syllables, and a restrained separately animated blink; they do **not** establish which animation software or original rig the video's creator used. The source video provides rendered pixels only—not its project files, layers, or landmark data.

## Why v4 was wrong

The old mouth atlas was rendered at 4x resolution for antialiasing but **drew the top and bottom lip contours using unscaled coordinates**. Thus the oral cavity showed as a disconnected dark stripe without its intended outline. This concrete bug made the previous mouth look like a horizontal moustache or smear, especially in wide shots.

## v5 technique

1. Preserve the original Max/Mia image and its alpha.
2. Mask and inpaint only the tiny existing smile. Keep nearby cheek/painterly shading.
3. Draw **closed, soft, E/smile-teeth, A/wide, O/round, F/V, and idle** visemes on the face at native coordinates **using consistently supersampled 4x lip geometry**.
4. Use shaded oral cavities, a narrow upper-teeth strip, feathered edges, subtle lower-lip highlights, and no rectangular hard seam.
5. Map Kokoro ONNX phoneme timings from `timeline.json` to the visemes. Extend very short vowel intervals to visible 3-frame holds; keep M/B/P true mouth closure.
6. Preserve independent blink poses, speaker-focused close-ups, and the 12 background locations every ten seconds.

This is a **reference-technique approximation**, not an exact recreation of original artwork or per-frame motion. For exact reproduction of the reference's lip graphics, a properly layered rig or artist-authored seven-pose mouth atlas aligned to each character is necessary. No evidence in the uploaded MP4 proves any specific third-party face animation app.
