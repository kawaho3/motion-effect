# Related history — do not conflate the projects

**Background** (this repo): isolate a character's existing motion into a silent transparent video.

**Flower Friends** (related 2D animation experiment): an earlier 30-second flower-name lesson with funny conversation between Mia and Max, teaching rose, sunflower and tulip. Features detailed still artwork, blinking/lip swaps, slow camera motion, local Kokoro ONNX voices, subtitles and custom audio post-processing. Recovered source is in examples/flower-friends/, but aligned PNGs, original WAV and rendered output are not committed. The example is **not** a foreground matting pipeline. See its ORIGINAL_README.md. Prior visual reference: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MjZdAeJbITQ

**Reed House** (separate Blender series pipeline): prior work produced 10 episode scripts, rig/set prototypes, a Blender technical pilot, recovery tools and planning. The checklist stated 0 of 10 final full episodes complete. This is *not* the scope or deliverable of Background.

Potential shared ideas: temporal alignment, image/keyframe organization, FFmpeg, audio omission/polishing, frame validation and reproducible exports. Avoid importing unrelated media or dependencies without a use case and licensing review.
