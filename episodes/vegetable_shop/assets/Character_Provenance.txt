VEGETABLE ADVENTURE — CHARACTER ART ASSET PROVENANCE
===================================================
Max_Standing.png and Mia_Standing.png were cropped from an original AI-generated
transparent watercolor character pair produced for this project, referring to
the existing Max and Mia character design. Their alpha masks have been cleaned
and independently verified as non-opaque. The sprites are NOT third-party
stock photography or ripped copyrighted film frames.

Market_No_People.jpg was edited procedurally from the original project-owned
vegetable-shop Scene_Concept.jpg; it fills the old people regions by reusing
neighboring produce/foliage areas. Some seams remain and need artist cleanup.

Derived scenery in journey_backgrounds.py is generated from deterministic
Pillow drawing code, with no hosted API or paid image model needed at runtime.
All copyrighted third-party models, fonts, external samples and stock photos
remain excluded from this directory. Review rights before redistributing
model weights, reference videos or unrelated proprietary source media.
